Pour-over ratios and water temperature

Start at 1:16. That's 1 gram of coffee for every 16 grams of water, so a 20g dose takes 320g of water. Go stronger (1:15) or lighter (1:17) once the grind is dialed in, not before.

Bloom first. Pour about twice the coffee's weight in water (40g for a 20g dose), wait 30 to 45 seconds, then pour the rest in slow circles.

Water temperature depends on the roast. Use water just off the boil, around 96C, for light roasts. Drop to about 90C for dark roasts, which extract faster and turn bitter with hotter water.

Aim for a total brew time of 3:00 to 3:30. Faster than that usually means the grind is too coarse. Slower usually means it's too fine.
