POUR-OVER BASICS
(handout from the saturday class at the roaster, typed up)

RATIO: 1:15 to 1:17. Start at 1:16.
BLOOM: 2x the coffee weight in water. Wait 45 seconds. Don't skip it.
WATER: Never straight off the boil. Let the kettle rest 30 to 60 seconds, about 93C. Same for every roast.
GRIND: Medium-fine, like table salt. Change the grind first, and change one thing at a time.
TIME: 2:45 to 3:15 for a single cup.
BEANS: Best within 2 to 4 weeks of the roast date.
