# Open Questions

Things the library can't resolve on its own. Revisit whenever new raw material lands; close what's
been answered by moving it to the bottom with a strikethrough and the date.

Three kinds:

- **Contradiction**: two sources disagree, or a source contradicts something Carter believes
- **Gap**: a topic page has a hole that no raw material fills
- **Unknown origin**: a raw file whose provenance couldn't be determined

## Open

### Contradiction: water temperature for pour-over (2026-09-21)

[[pour-over-ratios-guide]] says it depends on the roast: just off the boil (about 96C) for light
roasts, about 90C for dark. [[roaster-class-handout]] says never straight off the boil, about 93C for
every roast. Carter's own note sides with neither: "not sure about the water temp part though." Both
sides are on [[pour-over-variables]].

What would settle it on his setup: two brews of the same light roast, same grind and ratio, one right
off the boil and one after a 30 to 60 second rest. Only Carter can run that.
