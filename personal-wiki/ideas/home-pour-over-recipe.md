His own position on brewing pour-over at home, built from his brew log and the note he left on one
capture. What the sources claim about the same variables is on [[pour-over-variables]], not here.

## The recipe he's using now

From the latest log entry (09-19): V60, 20g of coffee, 1:16 (320g of water), grinder on 20, kettle
right off the boil, a new bag of lighter roast. 2:55 total. His verdict: "bright but a little thin.
might try 1:15 next time."

The grinder numbers (18, 20, 22) are settings on his own grinder. The log doesn't name the grinder,
so they don't translate to anyone else's.

## What he thinks

**Grind matters most.** "grind matters way more than anything else i've changed. the ratio barely
moves anything." (`raw/brew-log.md`)

**Water temperature: undecided.** On the guide he captured, he wrote "not sure about the water temp
part though, the class said the opposite" (`raw/2026-09-18-pour-over-ratios-and-water-temperature.md`).
The two sources do disagree. See [[pour-over-variables]].

## How it developed

| Date | What changed | Result, in his words |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 09-12 | 1:16, grinder 18, kettle right off the boil | 3:40, "bitter and kind of dry at the end" |
| 09-14 | Same beans, grinder 22, kettle rested 30 sec | 3:05, "way better, sweeter" |
| 09-19 | New lighter roast, 1:16, grinder 20, kettle right off the boil | 2:55, "bright but a little thin" |

## What the log does and doesn't show

- **The 09-14 improvement had two changes behind it.** The grind went coarser (18 to 22) and the
  kettle rested 30 seconds, in the same brew. The log fits his view that grind matters most, but it
  can't separate the two. Logged in `_open-questions.md`.
- **No logged brew tests the ratio.** 09-12 and 09-19 are both 1:16, and 09-14 doesn't give one. So
  "the ratio barely moves anything" isn't drawn from these entries. If it comes from brews he didn't
  write down, the library has no record of them. Logged in `_open-questions.md`.
