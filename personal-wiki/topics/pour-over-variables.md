What the library's two sources say about each variable in a pour-over, where they disagree, and where
Carter's own view sits. Two sources, one of which can no longer be checked, so this stays `developing`.

## Ratio

**[[pour-over-ratios-guide]]:** start at 1:16 (20g of coffee to 320g of water). Only move to 1:15 or
1:17 once the grind is dialed in.

**[[roaster-class-handout]]:** 1:15 to 1:17, starting at 1:16.

The sources agree.

**Carter's view:** "the ratio barely moves anything" ([[home-pour-over-recipe]]). None of his logged
brews tests that. Two are 1:16 and the third doesn't give a ratio.

## Bloom

**[[pour-over-ratios-guide]]:** about twice the coffee's weight in water, then wait 30 to 45 seconds.

**[[roaster-class-handout]]:** twice the coffee's weight, wait 45 seconds, "Don't skip it."

Same amount of water. The handout's 45 seconds is the top of the guide's range. His log mentions bloom
once: "bloom was maybe 30 sec? didn't time it" (09-14).

## Water temperature

**Contested.**

- **[[pour-over-ratios-guide]]:** it depends on the roast. Just off the boil, around 96C, for light
  roasts. About 90C for dark roasts, "which extract faster and turn bitter with hotter water."
- **[[roaster-class-handout]]:** never straight off the boil. Rest the kettle 30 to 60 seconds, about
  93C, for every roast.

They disagree at both ends. For a light roast the guide goes hotter than the handout; for a dark roast
it goes cooler. Neither cites anything, and the guide can no longer be checked. Logged in
`_open-questions.md`.

**Carter's view:** undecided. "not sure about the water temp part though, the class said the
opposite." His one light-roast brew (09-19) used water right off the boil and came out "bright but a
little thin", which doesn't settle it either way.

## Grind

**[[roaster-class-handout]]:** medium-fine, like table salt. Change the grind first, and change one
thing at a time.

**[[pour-over-ratios-guide]]:** gives no grind size. It uses brew time as the check instead: too fast
usually means too coarse, too slow usually means too fine.

**Carter's view:** grind matters more than anything else he has changed ([[home-pour-over-recipe]]).
The log's evidence is the 09-12 to 09-14 step, where the cup went from "bitter and kind of dry" to
"way better, sweeter". That step also rested the kettle, so two things changed at once. The handout's
advice is to change one thing at a time. Logged in `_open-questions.md`.

## Brew time

**[[pour-over-ratios-guide]]:** 3:00 to 3:30.

**[[roaster-class-handout]]:** 2:45 to 3:15 for a single cup.

The ranges overlap from 3:00 to 3:15. His three brews: 3:40 (outside both), 3:05 (inside both), 2:55
(inside the handout's range only).

## Beans

**[[roaster-class-handout]]:** best within 2 to 4 weeks of the roast date. The guide doesn't cover
freshness, and the log doesn't record roast dates.
