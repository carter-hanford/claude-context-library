A one-page handout from a Saturday pour-over class at a coffee roaster, typed up by Carter. Six rules
of thumb: ratio, bloom, water, grind, time and beans.

## What the artifact is

A plain text file. The line under the heading, "(handout from the saturday class at the roaster,
typed up)", is Carter's note, not the handout's. Everything after it is the handout, word for word

**How it was reached:** no URL, so the link ladder doesn't apply. The handout doesn't name the
roaster, the instructor, or a date. Nothing in it is checkable (no product, version or price), so
this page is transcription and stays `seed`.

## Key claims

- **Ratio.** 1:15 to 1:17. Start at 1:16.
- **Bloom.** Twice the coffee's weight in water. Wait 45 seconds. "Don't skip it."
- **Water.** Never straight off the boil. Rest the kettle 30 to 60 seconds, to about 93C. Same for
  every roast.
- **Grind.** Medium-fine, like table salt. Change the grind first, and change one thing at a time.
- **Time.** 2:45 to 3:15 for a single cup.
- **Beans.** Best within 2 to 4 weeks of the roast date.

## Worth keeping verbatim

> "Never straight off the boil."

> "Change the grind first, and change one thing at a time."
