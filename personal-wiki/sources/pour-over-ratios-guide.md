## Key claims

- **Ratio.** Start at 1:16: 1g of coffee per 16g of water, so a 20g dose takes 320g. Move to 1:15
  (stronger) or 1:17 (lighter) "once the grind is dialed in, not before."
- **Bloom.** About twice the coffee's weight in water (40g for a 20g dose), wait 30 to 45 seconds,
  then pour the rest in slow circles.
- **Water temperature depends on the roast.** Just off the boil, around 96C, for light roasts. About
  90C for dark roasts.
- **Brew time.** 3:00 to 3:30 total. Faster usually means the grind is too coarse; slower usually
  means it's too fine.

## Worth keeping verbatim

> "Drop to about 90C for dark roasts, which extract faster and turn bitter with hotter water."

The guide's only stated reason for its temperature advice.
